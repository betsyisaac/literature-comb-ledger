Bridge is strictly ion-activity thermodynamics (59 mV/decade potentiometry on a quantity no perovskite platform measures) — the scout correctly declined to claim a crystallographic analogy (AgBr is rock salt, CsPbBr3 is perovskite, their facets are not counterparts).

Primary-source fidelity verified independently by two verifiers; both found the citation placement (not citation fidelity) is a defect that is fixable: the lead's causal thesis "constant pAg narrows the distribution" is not in the two cited patents (US 4,147,551 actually credits "avoiding direct precipitation of silver nitrate with the second halide," and US 4,251,627's invention is jet mixing) but is in the corpus — US 5,429,920 and US 5,151,357 state controlled double jet at steady pAg gives narrow distributions, and the counterintuitive ripening-agent-narrows-distribution claim is in US 4,683,192 / US 5,275,930 (thioethers as the preferred class). Verifier 2 further noted US 5,900,223 already carries "controlled double jet precipitation" with constant ion activity (pH ~14) into oxide perovskite nanoparticle synthesis in water, so the transfer-of-concept is less virgin than claimed; what is genuinely untouched is the halide-perovskite / non-aqueous case.

The refuting verifier (score 3) raised a mechanism/physics objection worth carrying: halide ISEs are aqueous devices with reportedly non-Nernstian Br response in aprotic solvents, in DMF/DMSO bromide is complexed as PbBr(3-/4-) so free-halide activity is not an independent driving force, and the timescales are inverted (double-jet runs 15-60 min; LARP nucleation is mixing-limited and over in <~200 ms). This is exactly what the proposed cheap check tests — a half-day ISE calibration in the actual solvent that may well not pass. A null here closes the lead for under $1000; a pass earns the follow-on syringe-pump feedback experiment.

Other carried caveats: Nature Synthesis 2024 (PMC11466857) concludes that "relying on supersaturation-growth rate relationship for indirect control of MAPbBr3 growth is inaccurate" in bulk evaporative single-crystal growth — the strongest extant argument against the driving-force handle, unrebutted. Neither cited patent gives an electrode type, tolerance band, or coefficient of variation. Scout confidence 0.4 (unusually low, honestly reported).
