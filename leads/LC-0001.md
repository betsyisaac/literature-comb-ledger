Beeson's July 2026 preprint, "Tiling a triangle into a prime number of congruent triangles" (arXiv:2607.23453), proves that for prime N, a triangle can be cut into N congruent triangles iff N=2, N=3, or N == 1 mod 4. The paper names Erdos problem #634. That means 19 (the specific case the problem page highlights) and every prime == 3 mod 4 above 3 are impossible, so the prime half of #634 is settled.
